01Timeline

Live now, coming up, and later today

The Timeline shows every Map Condition running right now with a countdown to its end, then what starts next and what comes later in the day. Each one runs for an hour on one map.

  • Active Now cards with live countdowns
  • Tap the bell to get notified when an event starts. Track up to 5 for free.
  • Pull to refresh any time

Know what's coming, and where

Events groups the schedule by condition. See when the next Night Raid or Harvester starts, and every map it's coming to.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is ArcTimers made by Embark Studios?

No. ArcTimers is an unofficial ARC Raiders (ArcRaiders) companion app and event tracker made by players. It is not affiliated with Embark Studios. ARC Raiders is a trademark of Embark Studios AB.

When is the next Matriarch or Night Raid in ARC Raiders?

Map Conditions rotate across the maps on a schedule, and each one lasts an hour. Open ArcTimers to see when the next Matriarch, Night Raid, Harvester or Electromagnetic Storm starts and on which map, with a live countdown. Tap the bell and you get a notification when it begins.

Does it cover Pendola Pass and Frozen Trail?

Yes. Pendola Pass, the map that opens with the Frozen Trail update, is tracked like every other map: Dam Battlegrounds, Buried City, Spaceport, The Blue Gate, Stella Montis and Riven Tides.

Where does the schedule come from?

From MetaForge's community ARC Raiders data (metaforge.app). The app refreshes when you open it, when you pull to refresh, and in the background so your notifications stay on time.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Your tracked events and custom alerts stay on your device. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. The app is in English and French.

How do I get in touch?

Email support@arctimers.com. Feedback and ideas are welcome.
