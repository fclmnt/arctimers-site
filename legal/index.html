3. What the app does

  • Timeline: shows active and upcoming ARC Raiders map events (Map Conditions) for each map, using schedule data from MetaForge, a third-party community service.

  • Trials: shows the weekly Trials, also using data from MetaForge.

02Privacy Policy

In short

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • Everything you set up in the app (tracked events, custom alerts, preferences) stays on your device. We never receive it.
  • The app downloads event schedules and images from MetaForge, whose servers see your IP address, as with any internet request.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your name, email address or payment details.
  • The only personal information we ever receive is what you choose to send us by email.

1. Who is responsible

Franck Clement, publisher of ArcTimers, is responsible for this policy and is the person in charge of the protection of personal information. For any privacy question or request, email arctimers@gmail.com.

This policy follows Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Quebec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector. For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, it also covers the information the GDPR requires.

2. Information stored only on your device

The app stores the following on your device so it can work. It is not sent to us or to anyone else:

  • the events you track and the custom alerts you create (event types, maps, lead times and on/off status);
  • the time until which custom alerts are paused, if you paused them;
  • whether ArcTimers Pro was active at the last check, so Pro features work before the App Store responds;
  • simple counters used to decide when to show Apple’s rating prompt;
  • cached copies of the latest schedules, Trials and images, so the app opens quickly and works offline.

The app reads your device’s time zone to display times locally. It does not send it anywhere.

Depending on your settings, iOS may include this app data in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

3. Data downloaded from MetaForge

To show schedules and Trials, the app downloads data from MetaForge (metaforge.app) and images from the servers MetaForge uses to host them. It does this when you open or refresh the app and, if Background App Refresh is on, periodically in the background to keep notifications up to date.

As with any internet request, those servers receive your IP address and standard technical information such as the request time and your operating system version. The app does not send any identifier, account information or personal data with these requests. MetaForge is an independent service and its own privacy policy applies to the requests it receives. We do not receive or have access to MetaForge’s server logs.

The link to MetaForge in the app’s Settings opens its website in your browser, where MetaForge’s own policies apply.

4. Notifications

All notifications are local notifications scheduled on your device by iOS. The app does not use a push notification server and does not collect a device token. You can turn notifications off at any time in the Settings app.

5. Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, email address, Apple Account details or payment information. The app checks the status of your ArcTimers Pro purchase on your device using Apple’s StoreKit, which returns a transaction record signed by Apple. This check stays on your device.

Apple provides developers with sales and subscription reports in App Store Connect. These reports do not identify you personally.
